# Agentic AI Sales Copilot

## Overview

The Agentic AI Sales Copilot is a sales productivity concept designed to help Account Managers and Inside Sales teams prepare faster, identify account risks earlier, and take better next-best actions across renewal and expansion opportunities.

## Business Problem

Sales teams often lose time manually gathering account details, checking renewal timelines, reviewing quote status, identifying blockers, and preparing partner or customer follow-ups.

This creates:

- Slower sales readiness
- Missed risk signals
- Weak follow-up discipline
- Delayed renewal action
- Poor visibility across account status

## Proposed Solution

A sales copilot that consolidates account data, renewal status, quote progress, customer risk signals, partner action items, and recommended next steps.

## Key Capabilities

- Account research summary
- Renewal timeline tracking
- Opportunity insight capture
- Risk signal identification
- Partner follow-up reminders
- Quote status visibility
- Next-best-action recommendations
- Customer conversation prep

## Value Delivered

- Reduces manual account research effort
- Improves sales readiness before customer and partner conversations
- Helps prioritize high-value and high-risk accounts
- Strengthens renewal risk visibility
- Improves follow-up discipline

## Relevant Skills Demonstrated

- Agentic AI thinking
- Sales process design
- Account management workflow
- Renewal risk management
- CRM-based decision-making
- Business transformation mindset
