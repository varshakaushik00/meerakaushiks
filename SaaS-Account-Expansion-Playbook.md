# SaaS Account Expansion Playbook

## Overview

This playbook outlines a structured approach to identifying upsell and cross-sell opportunities within existing SaaS accounts.

## Business Problem

Expansion opportunities are often missed when account teams focus only on renewal closure and do not actively look for growth triggers.

Common missed signals include:

- User growth
- Product adoption gaps
- Security or performance concerns
- New business units
- Cloud migration plans
- Support pain points
- Expiring infrastructure
- Partner-led expansion opportunities

## Expansion Framework

### 1. Account Review

- Current product usage
- Renewal timeline
- Contract value
- Support history
- Known business changes
- Stakeholder map

### 2. Expansion Triggers

- More users or teams
- Need for premium features
- Security requirements
- Performance limitations
- Compliance needs
- Hybrid or cloud strategy
- Consolidation opportunities

### 3. Customer Conversation

Key questions:

- What has changed in your environment since the last renewal?
- Are more users or teams relying on the solution now?
- Are there any performance, access, or security gaps?
- Are you planning any IT or cloud changes in the next 6-12 months?
- Would it help to review options beyond a like-for-like renewal?

### 4. Partner Motion

- Align with partner on customer direction
- Confirm quote preference
- Identify rebate or deal-registration needs
- Track technical validation
- Push for closure before risk window

## Business Value

- Supports 25%+ upsell and cross-sell growth
- Improves customer discovery
- Strengthens account planning
- Helps shift renewals into expansion conversations
- Creates better alignment between customer, partner, and internal teams

## Relevant Skills Demonstrated

- Strategic account management
- Expansion selling
- Customer discovery
- Partner-led sales
- Commercial positioning
- Objection handling
