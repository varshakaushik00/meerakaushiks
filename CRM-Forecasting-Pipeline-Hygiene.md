# CRM Forecasting & Pipeline Hygiene Framework

## Overview

This framework improves forecast accuracy, opportunity discipline, pipeline visibility, and sales-cycle control using CRM-driven insights.

## Business Problem

Poor CRM hygiene creates inaccurate forecasts, weak leadership visibility, delayed deal action, and missed closure opportunities.

Common issues include:

- Incorrect opportunity stage
- Missing next steps
- Outdated close dates
- No risk notes
- No partner action tracking
- No stakeholder visibility
- Poor quote-status updates

## Framework

### 1. Opportunity Hygiene

Every opportunity should have:

- Correct stage
- Updated close date
- Current renewal value
- Quote status
- Customer decision status
- Partner owner
- Next action
- Risk flag

### 2. Forecast Review

Weekly review should answer:

- What is closing this month?
- What is blocked?
- What needs leadership support?
- Which accounts are at risk?
- What changed from last week?
- Which deals need partner escalation?

### 3. Risk Classification

| Risk Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Customer aligned, quote shared, closure likely |
| Medium | Waiting on customer / partner action |
| High | Pricing objection, delayed response, competitive risk, expiry pressure |

### 4. Action Discipline

Every high-value or high-risk opportunity should have:

- Owner
- Clear next step
- Deadline
- Escalation path
- Customer / partner follow-up plan

## Business Value

- Supports forecast accuracy
- Reduces sales cycle time by up to 20%
- Improves leadership visibility
- Strengthens closure readiness
- Helps prioritize high-value opportunities
