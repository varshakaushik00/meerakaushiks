# Renewal Risk Tracker

## Overview

The Renewal Risk Tracker is a structured framework for tracking customer renewal readiness, commercial blockers, quote status, partner follow-ups, and closure risk.

## Business Problem

Renewals can fail or get delayed when teams do not have clear visibility into:

- Expiry dates
- Quote status
- Customer objections
- Partner follow-ups
- Pricing blockers
- Stakeholder delays
- Internal approvals

## Tracker Structure

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Customer Name | Account identification |
| Renewal Date | Timeline visibility |
| Renewal Value | Revenue prioritization |
| Quote Status | Commercial progress |
| Risk Level | High / Medium / Low |
| Customer Objection | Reason for delay |
| Partner Action | Required follow-up |
| Internal Blocker | Pricing, legal, operations, approval |
| Next Step | Clear ownership |
| Target Closure Date | Closure discipline |

## Business Value

- Supports 95%+ renewal-rate discipline
- Improves renewal prioritization
- Reduces last-minute closure risk
- Creates visibility across partner and customer actions
- Helps leadership quickly identify stuck opportunities
