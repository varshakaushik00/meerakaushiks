# Sales Leadership & Escalation Framework

## Overview

This framework shows how I approach team support, escalation handling, RCA, workflow discipline, and performance enablement.

## Business Problem

Teams lose productivity when escalations are handled reactively without clear ownership, documentation, prioritization, or root-cause review.

Common issues include:

- Repeated escalations
- Delayed resolution
- Poor ownership
- No RCA
- Knowledge gaps
- Weak team communication
- Lack of process documentation

## Leadership Approach

### 1. Clarify the Issue

- What happened?
- Who is impacted?
- What is the urgency?
- What is the revenue or customer risk?
- What action has already been taken?

### 2. Assign Ownership

- Customer owner
- Internal owner
- Partner owner
- Escalation owner
- Timeline owner

### 3. Resolve and Communicate

- Set expectation clearly
- Update all stakeholders
- Track blockers
- Confirm closure
- Document outcome

### 4. Root Cause Analysis

- Why did the issue happen?
- Was it process, data, communication, training, or ownership?
- How do we prevent repeat issues?
- What SOP or tracker needs updating?
